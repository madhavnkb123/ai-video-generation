AI VIDEO CAMERA CHEAT SHEET

Close-up: cinematic close-up of the character's face
Wide: wide establishing cinematic shot
Dolly In: slow cinematic dolly-in toward the character
Dolly Out: slow dolly-out revealing the environment
Tracking: smooth tracking shot following the character
Crane: cinematic crane shot rising above the scene
Aerial: high-altitude aerial cinematic shot
POV: first-person POV camera perspective
Over Shoulder: cinematic over-the-shoulder shot
Low Angle: dramatic low-angle cinematic shot

Useful lens terms:
24mm = wide environment
35mm = cinematic natural perspective
50mm = natural portrait
85mm = close portrait / shallow depth of field
