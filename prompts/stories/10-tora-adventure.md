# Tora's Adventure

Tora is a playful one-year-old child with curly hair, bright expressive eyes and a cheerful personality. Tora lives with mummy, papa and a funny loyal dog.

One morning, Tora discovers a mysterious glowing object in the garden. The dog follows Tora on a funny adventure. Together they discover a tiny magical world hidden under the garden.

Scenes: Garden → Glowing object → Dog follows → Hidden world → Magical adventure.
