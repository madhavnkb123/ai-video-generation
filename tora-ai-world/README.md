# Tora's Adventure — 1,000 Episode Universe

Tora is a playful one-year-old child with curly hair, bright expressive eyes and a cheerful personality. Tora lives with Mummy, Papa and a funny loyal dog named Buddy.

One morning, Tora discovers a mysterious glowing object in the garden. Buddy follows Tora on a funny adventure, and together they discover a tiny magical world hidden under the garden.

## Universe Format
20 seasons × 50 episodes = 1,000 episodes.

## Production Template
Every episode should contain:
- Episode number and title
- Story synopsis
- Character list
- Location
- Scene-by-scene prompts
- Dialogue
- Sound effects
- Music direction
- Camera directions
- Thumbnail prompt
- YouTube title and description

## Character Bible

### Tora
Age: 1 year; curly hair; bright expressive eyes; playful, curious and cheerful; toddler-like movement.

### Buddy
Tora's loyal dog; funny, mischievous, energetic and protective.

### Mummy
Caring, intelligent and patient.

### Papa
Funny, adventurous and supportive.

### Magical Characters
Lumi — tiny magical-world guide.
Mimi — energetic magical squirrel.
Bobo — funny magical rabbit.
Ollie — wise magical owl.

## Episode Formula
HOOK → DISCOVERY → FUNNY PROBLEM → ADVENTURE → CHALLENGE → BUDDY COMEDY → SOLUTION → MAGICAL DISCOVERY → HAPPY ENDING → NEXT-EPISODE TEASER
